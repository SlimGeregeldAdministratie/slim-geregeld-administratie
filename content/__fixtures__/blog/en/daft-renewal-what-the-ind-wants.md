## What does the IND ask for at renewal?

A balance sheet and a profit and loss account for **two financial years**. See the [renewal page](/daft/renewal/).

1. Form 7535
2. Balance sheet
3. Profit and loss account

## How do you calculate owner's equity?

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Assets | €5,000 |
| Tax payable | €1,000 |
| Owner's equity | €4,000 |
