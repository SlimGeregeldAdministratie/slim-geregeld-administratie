You do not automatically need a holding company above your Dutch private limited company (besloten vennootschap, or BV). A [holding structure](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/de-holding-bv-uitgelegd/) can help separate assets from business risks and prepare for a sale. I start by asking which assets you want to separate and what you plan to do next.

## When would I consider a holding company worthwhile?

I consider a holding worth investigating when you can identify a specific purpose. That might involve accumulated reserves, a future business partnership or selling your company.

My first question is what remains after your salary, taxes and investments. Then I ask how much cash the business needs to keep available. Bring your latest annual accounts (jaarrekening) and an updated cash flow forecast (liquiditeitsbegroting).

I start with those figures before discussing a structure. Write down which assets you want to separate, their future purpose and the risks involved. That gives me something concrete to assess against your business plans.

## Will a holding reduce your corporate income tax?

A holding leaves corporate income tax (vennootschapsbelasting, or vpb) on business profits in place. Across several client files, I explain that corporate income tax shifts to the holding within a fiscal unity. The tax on business profits remains payable.

A [corporate income tax fiscal unity](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/) (fiscale eenheid) attributes the subsidiaries’ results to the parent company. The parent files the combined return, allowing losses in one company to offset profits in another. Conditions include at least 95% ownership, profit entitlement, capital entitlement and voting rights.

The [participation exemption](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/deelnemingsvrijstelling/deelnemingsvrijstelling) (deelnemingsvrijstelling) can prevent another corporate tax charge on qualifying dividends and gains from selling a shareholding. For your holding, those benefits can therefore fall outside taxable profits. This exemption operates separately from a fiscal unity.

I ask for the calculation behind any proposed tax saving. It should identify the structure, each company’s profits and the tax treatment being applied.

## What can a holding protect, and what happens on sale?

A holding can own assets separately from the operating company (werkmaatschappij), where business activities take place. The [Dutch Chamber of Commerce](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/de-holding-bv-uitgelegd/) (KVK) explains how this can spread risks and support separate sales of business activities. Protection has limits, including situations where directors fail to follow the rules.

I want to see exactly which assets you intend to separate. Include the associated debts and obligations. A company chart alone gives me too little information to assess your exposure.

When you later distribute dividends from the holding to yourself, a substantial shareholding (aanmerkelijk belang) brings those payments into [box 2](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/), the personal income tax category for that shareholding. Money held by your company and money paid into your personal account therefore involve separate tax steps.

## Does adding a holding remove your director salary requirement?

Your director and major shareholder salary (DGA-loon) remains relevant when you work for a BV in which you hold a substantial interest. Adding another company does not by itself remove that requirement.

The [customary salary rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/) (gebruikelijkloonregeling) consider comparable employment, the highest salary within connected companies and the EUR 58,000 benchmark for 2026. The highest applicable amount governs your salary. You must substantiate a lower comparable salary if you want to use that amount.

I assess your salary separately from the holding decision. I need to understand your duties and the time you spend on them. Record that work before setting your salary.

[Payroll taxes](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/rechtsvorm/besloten-vennootschap-bv) (loonheffingen) on the director salary also remain part of the administration. An additional BV provides no automatic reason to close your payroll records.

## What changes with and without a holding?

Adding a holding adds a separate BV to your structure. I compare tax, salary and accounting obligations individually so you can see what requires attention.

| Item | Without a holding | With a holding |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate income tax return | Your BV files its return | The parent files within a fiscal unity, subject to the [conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/fiscale_eenheid_vennootschapsbelasting/) |
| Director salary | Customary salary rules apply where relevant | The same [salary rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/) remain relevant |
| Dividends paid to you personally | Taxable where you hold a substantial interest | Distributions from the holding also fall within [box 2](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_2/) |
| Annual accounts | Accounts for your BV | The holding also has [annual accounting obligations](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-besloten-vennootschap-bv/) as a BV |

I use this comparison as a checklist. A combined tax return does not explain all the work needed for separate company records. Ask for the annual accounting work to be specified per BV when comparing costs.

## How do you add a holding above an existing BV?

I start with the current share ownership and the tax consequences of transferring it. A [share transfer](https://www.kvk.nl/verkopen-en-overnemen/de-overdracht-van-je-bedrijf/) requires a civil-law notary (notaris), and selling personally held shares can generate a taxable box 2 gain. Have the proposed route assessed before transferring ownership.

The [legal entity and business continue to exist](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_wijzigen_of_beeindigen/uw_onderneming_wijzigt_van_rechtsvorm/) following a share transfer. Transferring shares therefore does not, by itself, end your existing BV’s earlier obligations.

I suggest preparing the decision in this order.

1. Write down what you want the holding to achieve.
2. Gather annual accounts, current figures, shareholder details and financing agreements.
3. Have the tax route and ongoing accounting work assessed before arranging incorporation.
