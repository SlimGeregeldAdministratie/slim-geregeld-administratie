Since April 2024, the Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) has approved [first DAFT applications without an opening balance upfront](/daft/), giving new permit holders six months to register their business, open a business bank account and deposit the investment. That means approval can come before you finish arranging the business side of your move. The EUR 4,500 investment still matters, and the opening balance sheet (openingsbalans) remains evidence of that investment. The change concerns when you arrange and document the setup.

## What do the six months after DAFT approval cover?

The six-month period covers registration with the Chamber of Commerce (KVK), opening the business account and making the investment. Several applicants confirmed receiving six months to arrange registration, banking and the deposit. Over a 12-month period, 10 people asked for an opening balance for the IND, including questions about preparation, signatures, timing and cost. Those questions reflect the overlap between the [changed first-application process and the financial records you still need](/daft/).

The practical sequence starts with your citizen service number (BSN), followed by KVK registration, the business account and finally the deposit. These are connected tasks, so it helps to understand the sequence before focusing on the final bank transfer. In my role as [DAFT specialist at Slim Geregeld](/en/), I guide applicants until their file is ready to sign. Kyle Bouwmeester prepares the balance and signs it.

## Why does an opening balance still matter?

The opening balance remains evidence of the deposit and the starting point for your later financial records. It is also needed at renewal, during an IND check, when changing your residence purpose to DAFT, and often when dealing with a bank. The distinction between a first application and a later review matters because the [renewal documentation looks beyond the initial transfer](/en/blog/daft-renewal-balance-sheet-requirements/). Keeping the opening position gives you a starting figure against which later changes can be explained.

For renewal, the relevant records include a balance sheet (balans) and a profit and loss statement (winst-en-verliesrekening) covering the current and previous financial years. A bank statement alone is insufficient under the requirements described in forms 7035 and 7535. The [financial statements need preparation or review by an authorised independent expert](/daft/renewal/). This is why keeping evidence from the beginning has a practical consequence when the renewal file is assembled.

## How does the EUR 4,500 investment change after the deposit?

The EUR 4,500 is business equity (eigen vermogen), calculated as assets minus debts. Your bank balance alone does not show that equity. Equipment purchases, profit and money taken out for personal use affect different parts of that calculation. The recurring [DAFT capital questions](/djtt/) include whether equipment counts and what happens when private withdrawals exceed profit. A useful example therefore follows the money beyond the first deposit.

The Dutch Tax Administration explains that [private contributions increase business capital, while private withdrawals reduce it](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/privestortingen_en_priveonttrekkingen). A contribution is separate from business profit, and a withdrawal for personal use is separate from a deductible business expense. That distinction lets you see why putting money back into the business can restore equity without creating turnover.

This pattern comes up in several files. A worked example with round amounts:

| Stage after approval | Movement | Business cash | Equipment | Business equity |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Setup period | Deposit starting capital | EUR 4,500 | EUR 0 | EUR 4,500 |
| After the deposit | Buy equipment for EUR 1,000 | EUR 3,500 | EUR 1,000 | EUR 4,500 |
| Later trading | Earn EUR 500 profit, received in cash | EUR 4,000 | EUR 1,000 | EUR 5,000 |
| Personal withdrawal | Take out EUR 1,000 | EUR 3,000 | EUR 1,000 | EUR 4,000 |
| Capital restored | Contribute another EUR 500 | EUR 3,500 | EUR 1,000 | EUR 4,500 |

The equipment purchase changes the mix of assets in this example, leaving total equity unchanged at that point. The personal withdrawal then takes equity below EUR 4,500, despite the business having earned a profit. The additional contribution brings the example back to its opening equity level.

## How should you record costs paid before the business account exists?

Business costs paid personally before the business account exists are recorded as business costs with a corresponding capital contribution. This situation comes up repeatedly among applicants arranging their first account. A spreadsheet is a normal starting point, and [Moneybird is not required to prepare a balance](/en/). The useful starting record connects the purchase, the payment and its business purpose, so the contribution can be understood when the balance is prepared.

A transfer into the new account does not explain purchases already paid from another account. Keeping those transactions visible helps connect the setup period to the opening financial position. For you, the practical question is whether the records show both what the business acquired and how you funded it.

## What should you take from the change since April 2024?

The clear message is that approval and completion of the business setup now fall at different points in the first-application process. The opening balance retains its role as evidence, even when it was absent from the initial application. Later, [renewal involves financial records covering two financial years](/en/blog/daft-renewal-balance-sheet-requirements/), so the deposit is the beginning of the financial history you will need to explain.

Start your setup record with the capital transfer and the business purchases you have already paid personally. Keep that record connected to the opening balance Kyle prepares and signs. The six-month setup period changes the timing of registration, banking and investment; your financial records still need to show what happened to the capital.
