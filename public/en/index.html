The same rules for both treaties

On the IND application form the two routes sit side by side, as purpose 453 and purpose 510, with identical requirements.

€4,500 of equity

Not €4,500 in the bank, but €4,500 of owner's equity on your balance sheet. For an NV the IND asks for €11,250.

Signed by an authorised expert

An independent external expert has to check and sign the figures. A bookkeeper is on the IND's own list, so you do not need a registeraccountant.

Two years, then five

The first permit runs for two years. To renew it for five, the IND wants two years of figures showing the capital stayed in the business.

Sources: IND application form 7524 and appendix 7611, checked on 21 October 2026.
