Why you need this document

The Dutch American Friendship Treaty gives US citizens a straightforward route to a Dutch residence permit as a self-employed entrepreneur. One financial condition comes with it.

The IND requires €4,500 equity

Your Dutch business needs at least €4,500 of invested capital, and it has to stay in the business for as long as you hold the permit.

Proof must be signed by a professional

A bank statement alone is not enough. The IND wants an opening balance sheet drawn up and signed by a Dutch bookkeeper or accountant registered with the Tax Administration.

Most firms will not do it as a one-off

Frequently asked questions

What is the opening balance sheet the IND asks for?

For a DAFT residence permit the IND wants proof that you invested at least €4,500 of equity in your Dutch business. That proof is an opening balance sheet: a one-page financial statement showing what you put into the company, drawn up and signed by a Dutch bookkeeper or accountant, submitted together with a bank statement.

Who is allowed to sign it?

A Dutch administrateur or accountant registered with the Dutch Tax Administration. The registration the IND paperwork asks for is a Becon number. Slim Geregeld is Becon-registered, so your balance sheet is signed by a professional the IND recognises.

Do I need to deposit the €4,500 before you can start?

The deposit needs to be visible on a Dutch business bank account before the final signed version can be issued, because the balance sheet has to match your bank statement. You can already submit your request and we prepare everything in parallel, so the signed PDF follows as soon as your deposit shows.

Can the investment be more than just cash?

Yes. Equipment, a laptop, cameras or other business assets you bring into the company can be part of your equity. Cash alone is also fine. We list everything correctly on the balance sheet.

Do I need my KVK registration first?

The balance sheet is drawn up for your registered Dutch business, so a KVK number is needed for the final version. If you have not registered yet, submit your request anyway and we will time the delivery with your KVK registration.

How fast can I have it?

Standard delivery is within 2 working days after payment and documents are in. If your IND appointment is very soon, choose the rush option and you have the signed PDF the next working day.

Is €495 really the whole price?

Yes. €495 including Dutch VAT, no hourly rates, no intake fee, no subscription required. The rush option is €645. If you take the Compliance Plan the balance sheet is included in the monthly fee.

Do I need to visit your office?

No. The whole process is remote: you fill in the form, send your bank statement by email and receive the signed balance sheet as a PDF. It works from the US as well as from the Netherlands.

What happens when my DAFT permit needs renewal?

At renewal the IND checks again that the €4,500 equity is still in the business. That means keeping your books in order matters for your residence permit. Our Compliance Plan covers your VAT returns, income tax and the renewal paperwork so this is never a problem.

Can you also handle my VAT returns and taxes?

Yes. Slim Geregeld is a Dutch bookkeeping practice for freelancers and small businesses. Most DAFT clients move on to the Compliance Plan at €59 per month, which covers quarterly VAT returns, annual income tax and the equity statement for your permit renewal.
